About The Trust

Our story, our purpose, our people

Our History

Rooted in Purulia, Growing with Every Generation

Alok Dhara Trust was established with a simple conviction — that quality education should be within reach of every child in Purulia and the surrounding regions. What began as a single school has grown into a family of one school and four coaching centres serving over 1,500 students.

Over the years, the Trust has become a trusted name in academic excellence, competitive exam preparation, and character building.

Vision

To Be the Region's Most Trusted

To be the leading educational institution in the region, nurturing young minds to become responsible, knowledgeable, and compassionate citizens of India.

Mission

Accessible Excellence

To provide accessible, high-quality education through dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure, and a student-centric approach that balances academics with co-curricular development.

Chairman's Message

Building Character, Not Just Careers

"Education is not just about scoring marks — it is about building character, curiosity, and confidence. At Alok Dhara Trust, we are committed to shaping not just successful students, but good human beings. I welcome you to be part of our journey."

— Chairman, Alok Dhara Trust
